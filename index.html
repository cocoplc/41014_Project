41014 Sensors and Control · Assessment 4 · Track B

UR3 Vision-Guided Pick in MuJoCo

Finding and grasping a randomly placed ball with a wrist-mounted RGB-D camera

Chloe Sargiss · Hannah Murphy · Angus Tedeschi · Josephine Thu
University of Technology Sydney
Code Results Evidence

Overview

A ball is placed at a random position on a table. The UR3 moves to a look pose, localises the ball with its wrist RGB-D camera, estimates its own joint state with a Kalman filter, and uses state-space control and visual servoing to reach in and grasp it, bringing together sensing, estimation and control from 41014 in one closed loop.

System Pipeline

1

Scene Setup

UR3, table, gripper, wrist camera and a randomly placed ball in MuJoCo.

2

RGB-D Vision

Colour and depth images locate the ball and give its 3D position.

3

State Estimation

A Kalman filter estimates joint states from noisy measurements.

4

Control & Planning

State-space control and visual servoing drive the gripper to the ball.

5

Grasp

The gripper closes gradually and holds the ball securely.

Simulation Scene

Each run resets the scene with a new random ball position, and the robot starts from a fixed look pose so the ball is in view.

Vision Localisation

The wrist camera's RGB and depth images are combined to find the ball's position relative to the robot.

Results

Vision localisation was evaluated over many random ball positions by comparing the estimated position with the simulator's ground truth.
