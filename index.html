UR3 Vision-Guided Pick
in MuJoCo

Finding and grasping a randomly placed ball using a wrist-mounted RGB-D camera, a Kalman filter and visual servoing.

Chloe Sargiss/ Hannah Murphy/ Angus Tedeschi/ Josephine Thu
University of Technology Sydney
View Code Results Evidence
01 — Overview

Sense, estimate, control.

A ball is placed at a random position on a table. The UR3 moves to a look pose, localises the ball with its wrist RGB-D camera, estimates its own joint state with a Kalman filter, then uses state-space control and visual servoing to reach in and grasp it, closing the loop between sensing and action.

02 — Pipeline

From pixels to grasp.

Five stages take the robot from a fresh random scene to a secure hold on the ball.

01

Scene Setup

UR3, table, gripper, wrist camera and a randomly placed ball.

02

RGB-D Vision

Colour and depth locate the ball in 3D.

03

Estimation

A Kalman filter estimates joint states from noisy readings.

04

Control

State-space control and visual servoing drive the gripper in.

05

Grasp

The gripper closes gradually and holds the ball.

Simulation Scene

Each run resets with a new random ball position, and the robot starts from a fixed look pose so the ball is in view.

Vision Localisation

The wrist camera's RGB and depth images are combined to find the ball's position relative to the robot.

03 — Results

How well does it see?

Vision localisation was evaluated over many random ball positions by comparing the estimated position with the simulator's ground truth.
